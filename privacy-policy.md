# Privacy Policy for Flock In

**Last updated: August 10, 2026**

Flock In is an app for high school ASB volunteer events. Students sign in with their
school Google account, sign up for events, and check in at those events to record
volunteer hours.

This policy explains what the app collects, why, and who can see it.

## Information we collect

**Account information.** When you sign in with your school Google account, we receive
your school email address, your name, and your Google profile photo. Your profile photo
is displayed in the app but is not stored on our servers.

**Event participation.** When you sign up for an event or check in to one, we record
which event it was, the time you checked in, and the volunteer hours earned. This is the
record your ASB advisor uses to credit your hours.

**Notification token.** If you allow notifications, we store an anonymous device token so
the app can send you reminders about your events. It identifies your device, not you
personally, and is used only to deliver notifications.

## Location

The app asks for location access only while you are using it, and only to confirm you are
at an event when you check in.

**Your location never leaves your device.** The app compares your position to the event's
published location on your phone and sends only the result of that check. Your
coordinates are never transmitted, stored, or logged, and no location history is kept.

You can deny or revoke location access in iOS Settings. Events without a set location can
still be checked into without it.

## What we do not collect

We do not collect your contacts, photos, calendar, health data, browsing activity, or
advertising identifiers. We do not use analytics or advertising SDKs, we do not track you
across other apps or websites, and we do not sell or share your information with anyone
for advertising.

## How your information is used

Your information is used only to operate the app: to confirm you are on your school's
roster, to show you your events, to record your check-ins and volunteer hours, and to
send you notifications about events you signed up for.

## Who can see your information

School staff who administer the program, such as your ASB advisor, can see your roster
entry, your sign-ups, and your check-in records. Other students cannot see your
information. Access is enforced by security rules on our database, not only by the app.

We do not sell your information or disclose it to third parties, except to the service
providers below or where required by law.

## Service providers

The app relies on these services, which process data on our behalf:

- **Google Firebase** (Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Messaging): stores your account
  record, event sign-ups, check-ins, and notification token.
- **Google Sign-In**: verifies your school account.
- **Vercel**: hosts the administrative interface and the sign-up service the app calls.

Each has its own privacy policy governing how it handles data.

## Students and minors

Flock In is provided to students through their school and is limited to school Google
Workspace accounts on a roster maintained by school staff. It is intended for high school
students and is not directed to children under 13. We collect only what is needed to run
the volunteer program, and no information is used for advertising or profiling.

Parents and guardians who have questions about their student's information should contact
the school's ASB advisor or use the contact below.

## Test account

The app includes a demonstration account for app review and testing. It shows fixed
sample data, collects no personal information, and does not connect to any real student
record.

## Data retention and deletion

Records are kept while you participate in the program and for as long as your school
needs them for volunteer hour reporting. To request access to or deletion of your
information, contact your ASB advisor or use the contact below. Some records may be
retained where the school is required to keep them.
